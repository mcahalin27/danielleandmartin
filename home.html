11 June 2027  ·  Gloster House  ·  Black Tie Travel & Accommodation
Travelling There

We recommend renting a car from Dublin Airport — it's the most straightforward option and gives you the freedom to explore the Irish countryside while you're here. You can also fly into Shannon or Cork, but Dublin is the easiest connection. Gloster House is roughly 1 hour 30 minutes from Dublin, 1 hour from Shannon, and 85 minutes from Galway.

Where to Stay
Dooly's Hotel
★★★
Emmet Square, Birr, Co. Offaly

A beloved Birr institution dating back to 1747 and one of the oldest coaching inns in the country. Right in the heart of town on Emmet Square, with 17 cosy ensuite rooms and the atmospheric Coach House Bar — live music on Saturdays.

County Arms Hotel
★★★★
Railway Road, Birr, Co. Offaly, R42 Y568

Birr's only four-star hotel, set on a beautiful nine-acre Georgian estate built in 1809. Seventy contemporary bedrooms, The Springs Leisure Club with pool, sauna and spa, plus Hackett's Eatery and the Shaker Room cocktail bar.

Townsend House
★★★
Townsend Street, Birr, Co. Offaly, R42 TK88

Birr's top-rated guesthouse — an elegant 19th-century Georgian house with ten individually decorated ensuite rooms, a lovely tapas restaurant and wine bar, mature gardens and private courtyard parking.

11 June 2027  ·  Gloster House  ·  Black Tie FAQs

What time does the ceremony start?

The ceremony begins at 3:00pm. Shuttles depart Birr town at 1:45pm — we recommend hopping on the bus so you're seated in plenty of time.

What is the dress code?

Black Tie. We'd love to see everyone dressed to the nines — it's going to be a beautiful evening.

Is there parking at the venue?

Yes, parking is available at Gloster House. Full details to follow closer to the date.

Are children welcome?

We love your little ones, but the evening reception is adults only. More details to follow closer to the date.

Will there be a bus service?

Yes! Shuttles depart Birr at 1:45pm on Friday and the final bus back to Birr leaves at 3:30am. On Day Two, buses to Birr depart Gloster House at 7:00pm. Pick-up point details to follow.

Is there a Day Two?

Absolutely — and we'd love you to join us. BBQ and live music at Gloster House from 2:30pm on Saturday 12 June, followed by Ceol agus Craic at Craughwell's in Birr from 7:30pm.

What is the nearest airport?

Dublin Airport is the most straightforward, roughly 1 hour 30 minutes away. Shannon is about an hour and is a great option for those flying from the US.

What if I can't make it?

We'll miss you! Please let us know as soon as you can so we can plan accordingly.

Ireland's cultural capital. Buzzing streets, brilliant restaurants, trad music in every second pub and the famous Quay Street.

A beautiful harbour town between the Atlantic and the mountains. Exceptional seafood, cosy pubs and dramatic scenery.

🚗 Road Trips
Multi-day · West Coast

The Wild Atlantic Way

Ireland's epic 2,500km coastal route from Donegal to Cork. Even a single day along the Clare and Galway stretch is unforgettable.

1 hr · Co. Clare

Cliffs of Moher & The Burren

Ireland's most iconic natural landmark, with 8km of cliffs rising 200m above the Atlantic. Pair it with the lunar landscape of the Burren.

2 hrs 30 · Co. Kerry

The Ring of Kerry

A classic 179km loop around the Iveragh Peninsula taking in mountains, lakes and coastline. One of Europe's great drives.

25 mins · Co. Offaly

Clonmacnoise

One of Ireland's most important early Christian monastic sites, sitting peacefully on the banks of the River Shannon.
