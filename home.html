11 June 2027  ·  Gloster House  ·  Black Tie Travel & Accommodation

Getting There

We recommend renting a car from Dublin Airport — it's the most straightforward option and gives you the freedom to explore the beautiful Irish countryside. You can also fly into Shannon or Cork airports, but Dublin is the easiest connection. Gloster House is approximately 1 hour 30 minutes from Dublin, 1 hour from Shannon, and 85 minutes from Galway.

Where to Stay
Dooly's Hotel
★★★
📍 Emmet Square, Birr, Co. Offaly

A beloved Birr institution dating back to 1747. Right in the heart of town on Emmet Square, Dooly's offers 17 cosy ensuite rooms, the atmospheric Coach House Bar (live music Saturdays), and warm Offaly hospitality that has welcomed travellers for generations.

County Arms Hotel
★★★★
📍 Railway Road, Birr, Co. Offaly, R42 Y568

Birr's only 4-star hotel, set on a stunning 9-acre Georgian estate. Built in 1809, it combines old-world grandeur with contemporary design. 70 bedrooms, The Springs Leisure Club (pool, spa, gym), and Hackett's Eatery. A beautiful base for the weekend.

Townsend House
★★★
📍 Townsend Street, Birr, Co. Offaly, R42 TK88

Birr's #1 rated guesthouse on TripAdvisor. This elegant 19th-century Georgian house has 10 beautifully decorated ensuite rooms, a fantastic tapas restaurant, private walled garden, and hosts Martin & Lorraine who go above and beyond for their guests.

11 June 2027  ·  Gloster House  ·  Black Tie FAQs

What time does the ceremony start?

The ceremony begins at 3:00pm. Shuttles depart Birr town at 1:45pm. We recommend being on the bus to ensure you're seated in plenty of time.

What is the dress code?

Black Tie. We'd love to see everyone dressed to the nines — it's going to be a beautiful evening!

Is there parking at the venue?

Yes, parking is available at Gloster House for guests who are driving. We will share full details closer to the date.

Are children welcome?

We love your little ones but the evening reception is adults only. We will share more details closer to the date.

Will there be a bus service?

Yes! Shuttles depart Birr at 1:45pm on Friday and the final bus back to Birr is at 3:30am. Day 2 buses to Birr depart Gloster House at 7:00pm. Full pick-up point details to follow.

Is there a Day 2?

Absolutely — and we'd love you to join us! BBQ and live music at Gloster House from 2:30pm on Saturday 12 June, followed by a night of Ceol agus Craic at Craughwell's in Birr from 7:30pm.

What is the nearest airport?

Dublin Airport is the most straightforward — approximately 1 hour 30 minutes from Gloster House. Shannon Airport is about 1 hour away and is a great option for those flying from the US.

What if I can't make it?

We'll miss you! Please update your RSVP as soon as possible so we can plan accordingly.

⛳ Golf

Birr Golf Club

A charming 18-hole parkland course right on the doorstep. Perfect for a round before the wedding weekend kicks off.

30 mins · Tullamore

Esker Hills Golf Club

A highly regarded heathland course in Tullamore, consistently rated one of the best inland courses in Ireland. Excellent facilities.

1.5 hrs · Co. Clare

Lahinch Golf Club

One of Ireland's great links courses on the Wild Atlantic Way. A bucket-list round with stunning Atlantic views and classic links terrain.

2.5 hrs · Co. Kerry

Ballybunion Golf Club

Widely regarded as one of the finest links courses in the world. An iconic coastal course that belongs on every golfer's wish list.

🏘️ Towns to Visit
1.5 hrs · Co. Galway

Galway City

Ireland's cultural capital. Vibrant streets, world-class restaurants, traditional music in every pub, and the famous Quay Street. A must.

2.5 hrs · Co. Kerry

Dingle

A beautiful harbour town nestled between the Atlantic and mountains. Exceptional seafood, cosy pubs, and some of Ireland's most dramatic scenery.

2 hrs · Co. Cork

Kinsale

Ireland's gourmet capital — a charming coastal town with colourful streets, excellent restaurants, and a rich history. Perfect for a day trip.

1 hr · Co. Kilkenny

Kilkenny City

A beautifully preserved medieval city with a magnificent castle, craft scene, and some excellent pubs. Very walkable and easy to get to.

🚗 Road Trips
1 hr · Co. Clare

Cliffs of Moher

Ireland's most iconic natural landmark — 8km of dramatic cliffs rising 200m above the Atlantic. Easy day trip from Birr and utterly spectacular.

Multi-day · West Coast

The Wild Atlantic Way

Ireland's epic 2,500km coastal driving route from Donegal to Cork. Even a single-day stretch from Birr through the Burren and Cliffs is unforgettable.

2.5 hrs · Co. Kerry

The Ring of Kerry

A classic 179km circular route around the Iveragh Peninsula with breathtaking mountains, lakes, and coastline. One of Europe's great drives.

25 mins · Co. Offaly

Clonmacnoise

A UNESCO World Heritage Site — one of Ireland's most important early Christian monasteries, sitting peacefully on the banks of the River Shannon.
