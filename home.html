Things To Do

Making a weekend of it? Here are some of our favourite spots around Birr and County Offaly.

🏰

Birr Castle Demesne

Stunning historic gardens with one of Ireland's great telescopes — a perfect afternoon wander.

🌿

Slieve Bloom Mountains

Beautiful walking trails and spectacular scenery just a short drive from Birr.

🍺

Birr Town

A charming Georgian town with great pubs, restaurants and independent shops to explore.

⛳

Birr Golf Club

A beautiful parkland course — ideal for the golf lovers in the group the morning after!

⛪

Clonmacnoise

One of Ireland's most important monastic sites, set dramatically on the banks of the Shannon.

🛶

River Shannon

Boat hire and river walks along one of Ireland's most iconic waterways.
